## T1. Products of consecutive harmonic numbers: series  S_q = sum_k H_k H_{k+1}...H_{k+q} / (k(k+1)...(k+q))   [todo -> start first]
1. Who asked: W. J. Chen, V. Nguyen, "A series involving a product of four consecutive harmonic numbers", arXiv:2507.19502. Intro states Furdui & Sintamarian's
   Conjecture 1 (S_q is a rational combination of zeta(2..q+2)); says it "possibly fails when q=3" (their formula contains zeta(2)zeta(3)); the last page says
   "Perhaps we can learn more by computing the closed form for S_q (q in N)". Opened and read.
2. Still open? Web search found no general-q follow-up (one search, so weak evidence). Re-check before claiming.
3. Why: a family of Euler-type sums that people compute one q at a time; a general structure theorem (what the closed form always looks like) replaces a failed conjecture.
4. Doable: partial fractions + known Euler-sum/MZV reductions; weight q+2 <= 7 reduces to zeta products (q<=5). Check by 60-digit numerics.
5. Impact 3, chance 5 -> 15.
