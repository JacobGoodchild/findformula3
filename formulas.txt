[1] Riesz energy of three points on a circle (Fan)
Statement: for r>=2, B,C=e^{+-i phi}, phi in (pi/2,2pi/3], A=e^{i psi}, psi in [0,2pi-3phi]: the energy U_r({A,B,C}) is nondecreasing in psi (Conjecture 5.9); for odd N>=3, r>2 the vertex set P_N has energy 2^{r-1} sin^r(theta_N)/(1-2^{r-2}cos^r(theta_N)), theta_N=(N-1)pi/(2N), and an equilibrium measure on the three vertices 1, e^{i(pi-+pi/N)} with masses (2b^r-a^r, b^r, b^r)/(4b^r-a^r), a=2 sin(pi/N), b=2 sin theta_N (Conjecture 5.6; existence, not uniqueness).
Label: PROVED, given the three quoted inputs. Who asked: Fan, Conjectures 5.9 and 5.6 (opened PDF; see CITATIONS.md).
Checks: derivative formula and key lemma in sympy / 50-digit numerics; brute force over all vertex triples (odd N<=31, several r>2); two rounds of fresh referee review (both: complete modulo minor, fixes applied); an optimiser-based maximisation over measures on P_N (N<=13) by the referee.
Novelty: not found (a bot-posted GitHub issue claims a Lean 'result' for 5.9; unverified, see NOVELTY.md). paper Sec. 2; results/riesz_triangle.

[2] Gregory coefficients (Xu-Zhao)
Gr(l) = least N with (-1)^{n-1}G_n^{(l)}>0 for all n>=N, G_n^{(l)}=[x^n](x/ln(1+x))^l.  Conjecture 6.1 (0.47*3^l<Gr(l)<0.477*3^l, l>=5) is false: Gr(5)=113<114.21 (the paper's own value), and Gr(l)<0.47*3^l also for l=8,9,10 (rigorous) and l=11..14.
Gr(3..10)=11,36,113,346,1030,2995,8580,24303 (computer-assisted, rigorous, two independent implementations; tail via the authors' criterion with interval integration; takes their identity (6) and Lemma 4.1 as given). Gr(11..14)=68283,190698,530152,1468691 (numerical; Gr(11) also by an independent scan). r(l)=exp(pi cot(pi/l)) is the largest zero of h; Gr(l)/r(l) in [1.49,1.55] for 5<=l<=14: growth rate e, not 3 (observation; heuristic law Gr~e^{l+1-gamma} not independently reproduced).
Novelty: not found; OEIS has no match for 11,36,113,346,1030,2995,8580,24303. paper Sec. 3; results/gregory.

[3] Diagonal Pade denominators (Scardino / Fasi-Higham)
q_{m,m}(z)=sum_j (-1)^j C(m,j)(2m-j)!/(2m)! z^j. For every odd 3<=m<=361 all Taylor coefficients of 1/q_{m,m} are positive (m=1 trivial). Proof: certificate lemma (partial fractions + one dominant positive real zero + tail bound N0 + ball-arithmetic check of c_n, n<N0); 180 values of m, logs results/pade_positivity/rigorous_diag_*.log (no gaps, no failures); exact certification (squarefree, exactly one positive real zero, modulus gap) for all odd m<=361 in exact_checks.log. N0=372888 at m=361.
Law: k*(m)=smallest k with a negative coefficient: k*(5)=66, k*(7)=44, k*(9)=42, k*(19)=54 (exact rational), k*(109)=230, k*(209)=430 (exact rational near k*), and k*(m)-2m in {12,13,14} for sampled m in 109..1009 (interval arithmetic with bisection, monotonicity in k assumed) -- numerical.
Checks: fresh referee (scheme valid; completes m=3..11 and exact check c_n>0 for n<=3000 for m<=31); fresh re-run of m=101,201,301,(361) (see VERIFICATION.md). Novelty: not found. paper Sec. 4; results/pade_positivity.

[4] Harmonic products (Chen-Nguyen, Furdui-Sintamarian)
S_q expressed as an exact Q-combination of MZVs of weight <= q+2, q<=9 (program in exact rational arithmetic; asserts cancellation of divergent terms; referee: valid scheme). Closed forms in single zeta values (and zeta(5,3) from q=7): identified by PSLQ (150 digits), checked to 45 digits; q=1,2,3 known [Furdui-Sintamarian, Chen-Nguyen]; q=4..8 not found. S_4 and S_5 are in the paper; S_6..S_8: results/harmonic_products/S_table.txt. Fresh code: S_4 to 40 digits (referee); S_5..S_8 independent PSLQ (see VERIFICATION.md).
Observation (exact check q<=9): top-weight part = (zeta(q+2)+zeta(q+1,1))/q!.
Novelty: closed forms not found; method standard (Flajolet-Salvy; Xu-Wang). paper Sec. 5.
