[1] Products of consecutive harmonic numbers
Statement: S_q = sum_{k>=1} (H_k H_{k+1} ... H_{k+q}) / (k(k+1)...(k+q)) equals an explicit rational combination of products of single zeta values
 (plus zeta(5,3) from q=7). Full list in results/harmonic_products/S_table.txt. E.g.
 S_4 = zeta(2)/432 - 45zeta(3)/64 + 1799zeta(2)^2/8640 - 11zeta(2)zeta(3)/72 + 13zeta(5)/18 + zeta(2)^3/60 - zeta(3)^2/48.
Label: PROVED for q<=5 (exact algorithm to MZVs + classical weight<=7 reductions); q=6,7,8 VERIFIED (MZV identities weight 8-10 found by PSLQ at 150 digits).
Who asked: Chen & Nguyen, arXiv:2507.19502 (closing remark: compute S_q for general q); Furdui & Sintamarian conjecture (stated there) -- checked in the PDF.
How checked: (a) exact algebra asserts all T=H_K divergences cancel; (b) exact MZV combination == Euler-Maclaurin numerics to 1e-62 (q<=9); (c) final forms == numerics to 45 digits (q<=8); (d) q<=3 match the literature.
Extra (PARTIAL: proof sketch + exact check q<=9): weight-(q+2) part of S_q = (zeta(q+2)+zeta(q+1,1))/q! = (1/q!) sum_n H_n/n^{q+1}.
Novelty: q=1,2 (Furdui-Sintamarian), q=3 (Chen-Nguyen 2025) known. q>=4: NOT FOUND in two web searches (2025-2026 arXiv, general). "Not found" only.

[3] Conjecture 7.1 of Habib & Hildebrand (arXiv:2606.21145): for u''u-(u')^2 = e^{-x}P(u)-1 with P(0)=0 and P'(0)=P''(0)/2 (pi1=pi2), there are polynomials p_k with
 u = sum_k p_k(x) e^{-kx}, p_0=x, solving the ODE formally -- TRUE.  -- PROVED
Proof (results/painleve_series/README.md): the obstruction to solving for p_s is [x^2](e^{-sx} rho_s) (a residue); a local expansion at the zero of the truncated series shows it equals a
 combination of lower-order residuals times O(eps) quantities, hence vanishes at every order. Key identity e2 = (a2/a1) e1 + q0 pi1 a1/(1+a1) e0 checked symbolically; the explicit recursion confirms p_k exist (exact rational arithmetic) to k=40.
Who asked: Habib & Hildebrand, Section 7 "Conjectures", Conjecture 7.1 (opened PDF). Conjecture 7.2 (nonnegativity of coefficients when all pi_j>=0): PARTIAL -- reduced to coefficientwise nonnegativity of the right-hand sides rho_s (Lemma B proved: the linear solver preserves positivity); rho_s>=0 verified for 396 (P,s) cases, s<=12; symbolic p_1..p_4 positive in all parameters; exact check k<=40 (t^3), 30 (t^4), 20 (t^6); no counterexample.
Novelty: NOT FOUND (paper is from June 2026; one web search to be done). Related: Kitaev 2025 (cited there) treats Painleve III trans-series.

[5] Finch (arXiv:2503.00579) conjectured, with 100-digit numerical evidence, A(x/(1+x)) = B(x)+1 and I(x/(1+x)) = J(x)+1 for the Abel functions of x(1-x), x/(1+x+x^2), x/(1+x-x^2), x(1+x)/(1+2x).  -- PROVED.
 Proof: h(x)=x/(1+x) conjugates the maps (f_A h = h g_B, f_I h = h f_J), 1/h(x) = 1/x + 1, and the Abel functions are lim[1/f^n(x) - n -/+ ln n]; see results/abel_finch/README.md.
 Who asked: Finch, Section 1 ("A formal proof of both identities would be good to see someday"; opened PDF). Novelty: NOT FOUND (one reading of the paper; elementary). Caveat: simple argument, low depth.

[6] Finch (arXiv:2506.07625) conjectured exact values of the constant delta = g_EJ - g_ML between two normalisations of the Abel function of theta(x) = x + c1 x^{tau+1} + c2 x^{2tau+1} + ...: delta13 = -(3/4)ln 2, delta16 = -(2/3)ln 3, delta17 = -(5/9)ln 6, the families delta(k), delta(rho), the general Addendum III formula, and transcendental maps (sin, ln(1+x), Fresnel...). -- PROVED
 Theorem: delta = -(a/tau) ln(kappa), kappa = -tau c1, a = (tau+1)/2 - c2/c1^2. Proof = asymptotic inversion of g(x_n) = g(x_0) + n (results/abel_delta/README.md); reproduces all ~17 cases in the paper and 30 printed digits of delta13.
 Who asked: Finch, Section 'Delta conjectures' and Addenda ("Formal proofs would be good to see someday"; opened PDF). Novelty: NOT FOUND. Caveat: elementary; relies on the standard existence of the asymptotic expansion of the Abel function.
