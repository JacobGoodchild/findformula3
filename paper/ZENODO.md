**Description (suggested text):**
> We answer, or make progress on, several questions and conjectures posed in recent arXiv papers in classical analysis. (1) We prove two conjectures of Q. Fan on the Riesz capacity of three-point sets on a circle and of the vertex sets of regular odd polygons. (2) We show that a conjecture of C. Xu and J. Zhao on the sign thresholds of higher-order Gregory coefficients is false, and compute these thresholds rigorously (computer-assisted, interval arithmetic) for orders up to 10. (3) We prove, by computer-assisted ball arithmetic, that the reciprocal of the diagonal Padé denominator of the exponential has positive Taylor coefficients for every odd degree 3 ≤ m ≤ 361 — a case left open in the literature on a conjecture of Fasi and Higham — and describe numerically where positivity first fails for fixed m. (4) We give an exact algorithm and explicit values for series of products of consecutive harmonic numbers, short answers to questions of S. Finch on Abel's equation and on iterates of exponentials, two of the three open problems of H. Wiśniewska on Gurland's ratio, and numerical values of two completely monotonic degrees of Stirling remainders. Theorems, computer-assisted proofs and numerical results are clearly separated in the text; open problems are listed at the end.
