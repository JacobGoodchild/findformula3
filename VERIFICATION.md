# VERIFICATION.md — Phase C (3 Nov 2026)

Principle: the AI that found a result is a poor judge of it. So every PROVED result was sent, as statement + written proof only, to a **fresh referee agent that had not seen the earlier work or the code**; every numerical result was **recomputed from scratch by a fresh agent writing its own code**, by a different method where possible. Reports are in `referee_reports/`. Labels were downgraded wherever a check showed that a step was not rigorous.

## Late additions (3 Nov, ~10:40 UTC)
* **Final referee on the compiled PDF** (fresh agent, saw only `paper/main.pdf`; `referee_reports/final_pdf_referee.md`): verdict **minor revision, no fatal errors**. It re-derived the Riesz proof (Lemmas 2.4, 2.5, Prop. 2.6, reduction), the Padé certificate lemma (recomputed R and the gap for m = 3, 11, 101; k*(5,7,9) exactly), the Finch and Gurland propositions, Gr(2..5) by an exact rational scan, S_1..S_4 numerically, and compared the quoted conjectures with the arXiv PDFs. Not checked by this referee: Finch's papers, Chavan–Kobayashi–Layja, Flajolet–Salvy, Xu–Wang, Lanford–Yampolsky, Fasi–Higham (these were opened by me in Phase A).
