## Lessons from the verification pass (3 Nov 2026)
* **Scouting by one web search is not a novelty check.** Of the 13 first-night results, four turned out to be already in the literature once the papers' follow-ups were searched: Habib-Hildebrand Conj. 7.1 was proved by the same authors four days after the source paper (arXiv:2606.24182); the t-value conjecture 4.5 had been proved by Charlton (arXiv:2112.14613, Sec. 3.3, "to answer a question posed in [6]"); Finch's A/B identities were explained in his own Corrigendum; Samart's conjecture was refuted and explained in Zheng's paper (the paper is by Zheng, not Samart). Lesson: for every target, open the *latest* version of the source paper AND search for papers that cite it AND read the end of the PDF (corrigenda, addenda).
* **Transcribing statements from garbled PDF text is dangerous.** The Finch Addendum II formula and the t-value closed form were both first transcribed wrongly from `pdftotext` output; rendering the page (`pdftoppm`) and reading the image fixed it. Always render the page of a quoted formula.
* **Claims "from memory" ("Hoffman proved N=1,2,3", "Bjorck's theorem") must be traced to the source or reworded.** They were.
* **README numerical sanity lines can be wrong.** The Gurland README said "3000 terms give 1e-10"; the true figure at delta/(1+h)=0.98 is ~1e-55 (the 1e-10 figure matches ~500 terms and was an mpmath precision floor). Another README said N0 ~ 1.7 m^2 for the Pade thresholds; the data give N0/m^2 = 2.2..2.9 (slowly growing).
* **Precision floors in mpmath:** Hurwitz zeta of large order at dps=50 loses about 17 digits in the series test; use dps >= 100 for such checks.
* **Referee agents caught only presentation-level issues** on the four pure proofs they saw (all "complete modulo minor"), but in the harmonic-series case they correctly pointed out that "closed forms in zeta values" rests on PSLQ identifications of MZV reductions (so the label must be numerical, not proved), and in the Pade case they listed what must be certified exactly (squarefreeness, exactly one positive real zero, strict modulus gap), which the overnight logs did not record; `results/pade_positivity/exact_checks.py` now certifies these for all odd m <= 361.
* **Dead ends not pursued further:** an exact proof of the weight <= 7 MZV reductions needed for S_4, S_5 (could be done by exact double-shuffle linear algebra over Q); the all-m Pade diagonal statement (the gap rho_min/R -> 1 defeats the dominant-zero method); proving the Gurland inequality; Habib-Hildebrand Conj. 7.2.
